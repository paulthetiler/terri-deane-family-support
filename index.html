About Terri

Support starts with the person, not the diagnosis.

When ordinary childcare is not suitable, even straightforward things can become difficult. An evening out, school holidays, a wedding, a family day out or a trip away may all need extra thought.

My approach is relationship-led. I want to understand routines, communication, interests, sensory needs and what helps your child feel settled. Where appropriate, support can begin gradually so familiarity has time to develop.

Suitability matters more than taking every booking. If I don't believe I'm the right fit for your family, I would rather say so.

How it works

Simple, private and unhurried.

No booking engine. No anonymous rota. No pressure to commit before you've spoken properly.

1

Tell me what you need

Share a little about your child, your family and the situation you're trying to make easier.

2

Have a proper conversation

We discuss routines, communication, sensory needs, expectations and whether I may be a suitable fit.

3

Build familiarity

Where appropriate, shorter introductions can happen before longer or more involved support.

4

Agree the support clearly

Dates, boundaries, practical details and expectations are agreed before anything begins.
